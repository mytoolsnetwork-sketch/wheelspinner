Can't Decide What to Eat? Let the Wheel Decide!

The "what to eat" struggle is real. You open the fridge, stare blankly, close it, open it again — nothing has changed. Or you ask your partner and get "I don't know, what do you want?" for the fifth time. The what to eat wheel cuts through the indecision instantly with a random, fun, and completely commitment-free answer.

How to Use the What to Eat Wheel

  • Choose a preset — lunch, dinner, takeaway, healthy, comfort food, or quick meals.
  • Or add your own options — type your favourite restaurants, cuisines, or specific dishes.
  • Spin the wheel — get an instant random meal suggestion.
  • Don't like it? Spin again until something sounds good.

Why We Struggle to Decide What to Eat

Decision fatigue is real — by the time lunch or dinner rolls around, most people have already made hundreds of small decisions during the day. The mental energy required to evaluate food options feels disproportionate to the stakes, which is why "I don't mind, you choose" is such a common response. A random wheel bypasses the decision entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I eat for dinner tonight?

Add your favourite dinner options to the wheel and spin! If you're short on inspiration, try the Dinner preset which includes a variety of popular meal ideas to get you started.

Can I add my own restaurants or dishes?

Yes — just type your options into the text box, one per line. Add your favourite local restaurants, specific dishes you have ingredients for, or cuisines you're in the mood for.

What if I don't like the result?

Spin again! There are no rules. Keep spinning until something sounds appealing — or notice that your resistance to a result tells you something about what you actually want.

Is it free?

Completely free. No sign-up, no subscription — just spin and eat.
