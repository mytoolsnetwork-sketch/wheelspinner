What Is a Random Team Generator?

A random team generator is a free online tool that splits a list of names into balanced random teams instantly. It shuffles the names randomly and distributes them as evenly as possible across the number of teams you choose — no bias, no arguments, no time wasted.

How to Use the Random Team Generator

  • Enter names — type or paste one name per line.
  • Choose the number of teams — from 2 to 10.
  • Click "Generate Random Teams" — teams are created instantly.
  • Not happy with the split? Click again to regenerate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the teams truly random?

Yes — the generator uses a Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithm to randomize the name order before distributing them into teams. Every possible arrangement has an equal probability.

What if the numbers don't divide evenly?

The generator distributes names as evenly as possible. If you have 10 people and 3 teams, you'll get teams of 4, 3, and 3 — the extra person always goes to the first team.

Can I generate teams again?

Yes — just click the button again to get a completely new random split from the same list.

Is it free?

Completely free. No account, no subscription — just enter names and generate.
