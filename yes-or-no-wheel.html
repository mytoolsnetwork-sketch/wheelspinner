What Is a Yes or No Wheel?

A yes or no wheel is a free online decision-making tool that randomly returns either "Yes" or "No" when you spin it. It's the digital equivalent of flipping a coin — except more fun, more visual, and available in multiple modes including "Yes / No / Maybe" for those truly undecided moments.

When to Use the Yes or No Wheel

  • Small everyday decisions — should I order pizza tonight? Should I take that nap?
  • Breaking a tie — when two people can't agree, let the wheel be the tiebreaker.
  • Overcoming decision fatigue — sometimes you just need something external to push you in a direction.
  • Games & dares — spin to decide if a player gets a dare or a pass.
  • Teaching probability — a great classroom tool for demonstrating randomness and probability.

Yes / No / Maybe Mode

Not all decisions are binary. The "Yes / No / Maybe" mode adds a third option for situations where you're genuinely uncertain and open to either outcome. It's perfect for low-stakes decisions where any result is acceptable.

The Psychology of Coin Flips & Random Decisions

There's a famous trick for making decisions: flip a coin, and pay attention to how you feel when it lands. If you feel relief, that's your answer. If you feel disappointment, that's also your answer. The yes or no wheel works the same way — sometimes your reaction to the result tells you more than the result itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the yes or no wheel truly random?

Yes — each spin uses a random number generator that gives exactly equal probability to Yes and No (and Maybe if you choose that mode). There's no pattern or bias.

Can I use it for important decisions?

The yes or no wheel is best for low-stakes decisions. For major life choices, use it as a starting point — notice how you feel about the result — but ultimately rely on careful thought and advice from people you trust.

What's the difference between Yes/No and Mostly Yes mode?

"Mostly Yes" gives a higher probability to Yes — useful when you're already leaning toward yes but want a gentle nudge. Standard Yes/No is a true 50/50 split.

Is it free?

Completely free. No sign-up, no subscription — just spin.
