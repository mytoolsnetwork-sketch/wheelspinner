About WheelSpinner

Some decisions don't need a 20-minute debate — they need a wheel. WheelSpinner was built to make quick, fair, fun decisions in seconds, whether you're picking a winner, choosing a student, or just settling "what should we eat tonight?"

Our free online wheel spinner uses true randomization, so every option has an equal chance of being selected. Add your items, hit spin, and let the wheel decide — no app to download, no account needed.

🎯 Our Mission

To make randomness simple, visual, and genuinely fun to use — whether you're a teacher picking students, a host running a giveaway, or just someone who can't decide what to eat.

⚙️ How It Works

Add your options, click spin, and our randomization engine selects a result fairly and instantly. There's no limit to how many items you can add, and you can spin as many times as you like.

🛠️ More Than Just a Wheel

Beyond our main spinner, we've built dedicated tools for specific needs: a random name picker, a yes-or-no wheel, a classroom-focused spinner, a random team generator, and a "what to eat" wheel — all free and instant.

WheelSpinner is independently built and maintained. We don't require any personal information to use our tools, and we never sell your data. Have feedback or a feature idea? We'd love to hear it.
