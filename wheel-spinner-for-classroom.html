Why Use a Wheel Spinner in the Classroom?

A classroom wheel spinner removes the awkwardness of teacher-selected students and the unfairness of always calling on volunteers. When students know the selection is random, participation anxiety decreases — everyone is equally likely to be chosen, so there's no point in hiding. It also keeps the whole class alert and engaged, since anyone might be next.

How to Use the Classroom Wheel Spinner

  • Add your class list — type or paste student names, one per line.
  • Click "Pick a Student" — the tool randomly selects one name.
  • Already-picked students are tracked so you don't repeat — until you reset.
  • Reset when you want to start a fresh round.

Classroom Uses for the Wheel Spinner

📚 Question & Answer Sessions

Instead of calling on the same hands every time, spin the wheel to select who answers. Students stay focused because they know they might be chosen at any moment.

📖 Reading Aloud

Use the spinner to decide who reads the next paragraph. It keeps the class attentive and makes reading sessions more dynamic.

🎨 Assigning Roles & Tasks

Group leader, timekeeper, presenter — spin to assign classroom roles fairly and without complaints about favoritism.

🎮 Educational Games

Quiz games, review sessions, spelling bees — the spinner adds a game-show element that makes any activity more exciting.

🌍 Language Classes

Especially useful in language learning classrooms where you need to call on students quickly for oral practice. The randomness keeps everyone prepared.

Tips for Teachers

  • Display it on the projector — show the class the full name list so they can see the process is fair.
  • Use it consistently — students quickly learn that the spinner is how participation works, which reduces anxiety over time.
  • Combine with wait time — after spinning, give the student 10–15 seconds to think before answering. This improves response quality and reduces panic.
  • Use the reset function — track who has been picked so every student participates before anyone is repeated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, completely free. No account, no login, no subscription — just add your class list and spin.

Can I save my class list?

Your list stays on the page as long as you keep the tab open. For a saved list, bookmark the page after entering your names — they'll stay in the text box when you return.

Does it work on a projector or smartboard?

Yes — the tool is fully responsive and works on any screen size. Open it on your classroom computer and display it on your projector or smartboard for maximum engagement.

Can I use it for online classes?

Absolutely. Share your screen during a video call and spin the wheel live — students can see the selection happening in real time, which keeps remote classes as fair and engaging as in-person ones.
